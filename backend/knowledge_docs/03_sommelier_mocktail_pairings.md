# Nocturne Dhaka — Zero-Proof Sommelier & Botanical Mocktail Pairings

## §1.1 Zero-Proof Craft Philosophy
Nocturne operates a strictly zero-proof artisanal beverage program—zero distilled spirits, zero fermented wine, zero hidden alcohol extracts. Instead, our botanical cellar uses vacuum rotary distillation, smoked herbal infusions, and heirloom Japanese and Bangladeshi citrus to build complex tannin, acid, and aromatic finishes that rival classic sommelier pairings.
