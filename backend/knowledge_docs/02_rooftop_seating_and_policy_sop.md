# Nocturne Dhaka — Rooftop Seating, Acoustics & Reservation Policy SOP

## §1.1 Architectural Zones & Measured Acoustic Profiles
Nocturne occupies the 14th-floor penthouse of Banani Road 11 overlooking Banani Lake and the Gulshan skyline. Every seating zone is acoustically engineered and metered nightly:
- **The Skyline Rooftop (`rooftop`, Avg 52 dB):** Open-air glass perimeter wrapped in warm brass string lights and acoustic timber baffles. Tables `T-01` through `T-06` are 2-seater intimate round marble tables along the west glass railing. Ideal for quiet conversation, sunset proposals, and unobstructed skyline reflections (`48–54 dB`). Furthest from kitchen exhaust and clatter.
- **The Chef's Glass Kitchen (`main-hall`, Avg 64 dB):** Centered around the glass-walled white-oak hearth and live plating pass. Tables `T-07` through `T-10` sit directly opposite the culinary theater (`63–67 dB`, energetic ambiance). Tables `T-11` and `T-12` sit on the quieter south atrium colonnade (`55 dB`), balancing kitchen sightlines with easier conversation.
- **The Velvet Booth Lounge (`private-lounge`, Avg 46 dB):** Enclosed behind ribbed smoked-glass doors and heavy obsidian velvet drapes. Curved brass-trimmed VIP booths `B-01` through `B-06` seat 4 to 8 guests in library-grade acoustic serenity (`44–49 dB`), perfect for executive dinners and milestone celebrations.

## §1.2 Quietest Tables Away From Kitchen Noise
For parties of 2 seeking maximum quiet (`<= 55 dB`) away from the kitchen pass:
1. **`T-04` (The Skyline Rooftop — 48 dB):** Corner glass-railing sanctuary facing Banani Lake; our quietest outdoor 2-top, shielded by jasmine planters.
2. **`T-01` (The Skyline Rooftop — 50 dB):** North-west terrace edge with direct sunset and Gulshan tower horizon lines.
3. **`T-05` & `T-06` (The Skyline Rooftop — 51–52 dB):** Recessed candlelit alcove tables along the rooftop pergola.
4. **`B-01` & `B-02` (The Velvet Booth Lounge — 44–45 dB):** Available for intimate celebrations of 4+ guests (minimum 4 guests during peak 20:30 seating).

## §2.1 Two-Phase Seat Lock & Hold Timer Rules
Selecting any available table on the interactive floorplan triggers an atomic **10-minute distributed seat hold** (`600 seconds`). During this window, no other guest can double-book that table for the selected date and slot (`19:00`, `20:30`, `22:00`). Confirming your reservation locks the seat permanently and issues a scannable Digital Boarding Pass (`NOC-2026-XXXX`). If the 10-minute timer expires before confirmation, the table automatically returns to `AVAILABLE`.

## §2.2 Arrival Grace Period, Weather Cover & Dress Code
- **Grace Period:** Held reservations remain guaranteed for 15 minutes past the booked slot. Pre-ordered dishes fire on the hearth only when you check in at the host podium (or 10 minutes prior upon arrival confirmation).
- **Monsoon Retractable Canopy:** The Skyline Rooftop features a whisper-quiet motorized glass pergola that seals within 45 seconds during sudden Dhaka rain while preserving 270-degree skyline visibility.
- **Dress Code:** Smart Evening / Elevated Casual. Tailored shirts, evening wear, and smart footwear are welcomed; beach flip-flops and sleeveless athletic vests are politely declined.

## §2.3 Cancellation, Last-Minute Fees, No-Shows & Refunds
Nocturne balances guest flexibility with limited rooftop inventory (`18` total tables):
- **Free Early Cancellation (4+ Hours Before Seating):** Cancelling at least 4 hours prior to your reserved slot unlocks the table immediately and triggers a **100% automatic refund** of any paid deposit or pre-order back to your original bKash or card method.
- **Last-Minute Cancellation (< 4 Hours Before Seating):** Within 4 hours of seating, the **৳ 1,000** table hold fee is retained to cover held skyline inventory, while **100% of any unfired pre-ordered food balance above ৳ 1,000** is refunded automatically. Table-only deposits (৳ 1,000) paid at confirmation are forfeited under last-minute cancellation.
- **No-Show Policy (Past 15-Minute Grace Window):** If a party fails to arrive within 15 minutes of the seating time without notice, the host podium releases the table to standby guests (`NO_SHOW`). The **৳ 1,000** table hold fee is forfeited, and any surplus pre-paid course amount above ৳ 1,000 is refunded since the kitchen never fired the order.
- **After Check-In (`SEATED`):** Once checked in and kitchen preparation begins, reservations cannot be cancelled online. Please speak with the Maître d' directly at the host podium.
