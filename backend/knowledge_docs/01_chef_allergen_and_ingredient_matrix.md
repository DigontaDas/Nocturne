# Nocturne Dhaka — Chef Allergen, Halal & Sourcing Matrix (2026 Flagship)

## §1.1 Halal Certification & Meat Sourcing
All beef, lamb, duck, and poultry served at Nocturne Dhaka are 100% Zabiha Halal certified through audited supply partnerships. Our 35-day dry-aged Wagyu ribeye (`wagyu-ribeye-steak`) and Australian lamb rack (`herb-crusted-lamb-rack`) originate from Halal-certified Queensland and accredited export abattoirs. Zero pork derivatives, lard, alcohol-based reductions, or wine vinegars enter our kitchen—our pan glazes utilize aged pomegranate molasses, roasted bone marrow jus, and zero-proof verjus.

## §1.2 Dairy-Free & Ghee-Free Mains Protocol
Guests with lactose intolerance or strict dairy allergies must note that our signature **Wagyu Ribeye Steak** (`wagyu-ribeye-steak`, ৳4,650) is rested with cultured herb butter (`dairy`), making it unsuitable for dairy-free guests unless requested dry-seared over embers. **Saffron Porcini Risotto** (`saffron-porcini-risotto`, ৳1,850) and **Black Truffle Tagliatelle** (`black-truffle-tagliatelle`, ৳1,950) also contain aged Parmigiano and butter (`dairy`).

However, three main courses are **100% Dairy-Free by default** on dedicated clean-cast-iron stations:
- **Miso Norwegian Salmon** (`miso-norwegian-salmon`, ৳2,450): Oak-charred Norwegian salmon fillet lacquered in sweet white miso and certified gluten-free tamari over forbidden black rice and blistered baby bok choy. Naturally **Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, and Gluten-Free** (contains fish, soy, and sesame).
- **Roasted Duck Breast** (`roasted-duck-breast`, ৳2,250): Crisp-skinned Halal duck breast sliced over olive-oil whipped parsnip purée, charred parsnip batons, and tart blackberry-pomegranate glaze. Crafted with **zero butter, zero ghee, zero cream, zero gluten, and zero nuts** (Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Gluten-Free; zero major allergens).
- **Herb-Crusted Lamb Rack** (`herb-crusted-lamb-rack`, ৳3,450): Double-cut Australian Halal lamb rack crusted in rosemary, garlic, and toasted herb crumbs with roasted baby carrots and pomegranate jus. **Dairy-Free and Nut-Free** (contains wheat `gluten` in standard crumb crust; gluten-free herb crust available upon request).

## §2.2 Nut-Free & Celiac Cross-Contamination Guards
Nocturne maintains a **Peanut-Free** line and isolates roasted Sicilian pistachios strictly to the cold pantry for `artisan-fig-burrata` (which offers a "Omit Pistachio (Seed Crunch)" modifier). Color-coded cast-iron skillets and tongs separate dairy-basted Wagyu steaks from dairy-free salmon and duck stations. Guests noting celiac sensitivity receive tamari swaps and isolated prep boards.
